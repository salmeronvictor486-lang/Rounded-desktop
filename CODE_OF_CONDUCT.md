# Code of conduct

Rounded is a technical project with contributors from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want participating to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, stalking, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or ignoring maintainers' requests to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break these rules, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub (a private message to the repository owner, or a private security advisory if nothing else is available). Reports are handled confidentially.
